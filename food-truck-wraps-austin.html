Got Questions?

FOOD TRUCK WRAP FAQS

Preparing for opening day? Use the food-truck branding launch checklist to organize customer flow, menus, required notices, artwork, and future updates.

What does Canvas need to quote my food truck?

Share whether it is a truck or trailer, approximate dimensions, photos of every side and intended coverage. Mark windows, doors, exhaust outlets and existing graphics. Describe your artwork and identify the design approver; we will review surfaces, production and installation requirements before preparing a project-specific quote.

Should I choose a full wrap or partial graphics?

Partial graphics can focus your name, offer and contact details on selected areas while using the existing vehicle color. A full wrap covers more surface. Compare the options using photos and dimensions of your unit; the choice depends on coverage, paint condition and the approved design rather than a one-size-fits-all package.

How are service windows, vents and hot areas planned?

Photograph exhaust outlets, vents, hinges, doors and access panels, with service windows open and closed. We will review application areas and exclusions before design approval. Do not assume graphics can cover an opening or withstand direct heat; required service access must remain available.

What artwork should I prepare, including menu changes?

Organize your logo, brand colors, images and the menu wording that needs to appear. Separate permanent details from information that changes, and confirm the version before design approval. Describe available files in the form; we will confirm how to send them afterward.

What needs checking before installation and during care?

Disclose grease, old adhesive, repairs, corrosion, loose paint or contamination, and follow the project’s preparation instructions. Surfaces are reviewed before installation. Service life depends on the selected system, exposure, heat, cleaning and maintenance; request the applicable documentation and written terms rather than assume a fixed lifespan.
