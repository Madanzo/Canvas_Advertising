Premium Wrap Materials

We build premium durability into every wrap by exclusively using the industry's top-tier vinyl films and protective overlaminates.

Industry-Approved Premium Brands
3M

3M™ Wrap Film

Industry-leading wrap materials engineered for maximum conformability, vibrant color density, and unmatched warranty backing.

  • 3M Wrap Film Series 2080 Premium dual-cast vinyl featuring a protective film layer to prevent scratches during installation. Perfect for full color change wraps.
  • 3M IJ180Cv3 Controltac The gold standard for high-resolution printed graphics. Slideable and repositionable with Comply™ air release.
  • 3M Gloss Overlaminate 8518 High-gloss cast laminate that shields graphics from Texas heat, UV fading, and road debris abrasion.
Avery

Avery Dennison®

High-performance film with Easy Apply™ RS technology, allowing installers to slide, position and stretch for a flawless paint-like finish.

  • Supreme Wrapping Film SW900 Sleek paint replacement vinyl offering dual-layer protection. Durable up to 12 years with a clean paint-like look.
  • Avery Dennison MPI 1105 Premium cast print media offering superior print quality, stretchability, and paint-like gloss finish.
  • DOL 1360Z Overlaminate Ultra-thin, highly conformable gloss laminate providing exceptional UV defense and extending wrap lifespan.

WRAP & VINYL PRINTING FAQS

Clear answers for businesses, installers, and buyers comparing wrap printing companies in Austin and across the US.

Does Canvas Advertising ship wrap and vinyl printing across the United States?

Yes. We print wraps, decals, banners, window graphics, wall murals, fleet graphics, and replacement wrap panels in Austin, then ship finished vinyl print orders across the US.

Can I get vehicle wrap installation locally in Austin?

Yes. We provide Austin pickup and local delivery for printed wraps, fleet graphics, food truck panels, trailer graphics, boat graphics, storefront vinyl, and interior graphics. Local installation support can be coordinated when needed.

What can you print on vinyl?

We print full and partial vehicle wraps, fleet graphics, contour-cut decals, perforated window vinyl, storefront graphics, wall murals, floor graphics, banners, and white-label wholesale wrap prints for installers and sign shops.

Who should use Canvas Advertising for print-only wrap production?
