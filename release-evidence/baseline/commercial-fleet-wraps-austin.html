Got Questions?

WRAP FAQS

How much does a vehicle wrap cost in Austin?

The cost typically ranges from $1,500 to $4,500+ depending on vehicle size, wrap coverage (partial vs. full), and material type. Contact us for a free, precise quote.

How long do vehicle wraps last in Texas heat?

Service life depends on the selected film and laminate, installation, surfaces, exposure, cleaning, storage, and maintenance. Review the applicable product documentation and inspect graphics regularly.

Will a vinyl wrap damage my car's paint?

No, it actually protects your OEM paint from minor scratches and sun damage. When removed professionally, it leaves the paint underneath looking brand new.

Planning several service vehicles? Use the contractor fleet graphics checklist to organize unit data and approvals, then use the Texas commercial wrap maintenance guide to plan inspections, washing, repairs, and replacement records.
