Common Questions

WHOLESALE PRINTING FAQS

Everything you need to know about partnering with Canvas Advertising for your wrap production.

What is the turnaround time for wholesale printing?

Most wholesale print orders are ready for pickup or shipping within 2-3 business days. If you have an urgent rush job, let us know and we can often accommodate same-week or next-day turnarounds.

Do you offer white-label blind shipping?

Yes. We understand the importance of protecting your client relationships. We can ship your printed graphics directly to your installation facility or your client anywhere in the United States with blind, white-label packaging.

What vinyl brands do you print on?

We utilize industry-leading cast vinyls optimized for our HP Latex 800W, including 3M, Avery Dennison, and Orafol. We match the right film and laminate combination to the specific requirements of your wrap job.

Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

No. Whether you need a single replacement panel, a partial wrap for a food truck, or full graphics for a 20-vehicle fleet, we treat every order with the same priority.
