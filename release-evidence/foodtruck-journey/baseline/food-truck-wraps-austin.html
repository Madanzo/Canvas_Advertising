FOOD TRUCK WRAP FAQS

Preparing for opening day? Use the food-truck branding launch checklist to organize customer flow, menus, required notices, artwork, and future updates.

How much does it cost to wrap a food truck in Austin?

Food truck wraps typically range from $2,500 to $5,500 depending on the size of the truck or trailer, the number of windows, and whether you need a full or partial wrap. We provide free custom design with every wrap project.

How long does a food truck wrap last?

Service life depends on the selected film and laminate, installation, surfaces, heat and exhaust exposure, cleaning, storage, and maintenance. Review the applicable product documentation for the approved system.

Do I need to clean the food truck before wrapping?

Yes. Follow the preparation instructions provided for the project and disclose grease, old adhesive, repairs, corrosion, loose paint, sealants, or contamination. Final surface acceptance occurs before installation.
