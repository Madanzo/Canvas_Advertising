Neither 3M nor Avery is automatically best for every wrap. The appropriate film depends on whether the job uses pre-colored or printable media, the vehicle geometry, finish, approved ink-and-laminate system, exposure, installer requirements, and the current manufacturer bulletin for that product.

This comparison explains practical selection factors for color-change and commercial vehicle graphics in Austin. It does not imply that Canvas Advertising is a certified installer for either manufacturer, and manufacturer documentation should control warranty and application decisions.

Which film fits a color-change wrap: 3M 2080 or Avery SW900?

For custom color change wraps, the two standard-bearers are the 3M Wrap Film Series 2080 and the Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film SW900. Both are dual-layer cast films designed for vehicle application, but installer preferences and final aesthetics vary.

Avery Dennison SW900: Application Considerations

Avery positions Supreme Wrapping Film as a conformable cast film and offers Easy Apply™ RS adhesive technology. Installers should review the current technical data sheet for approved surfaces, recesses, application temperatures, post-heating, removability, and warranty requirements before selecting it.

3M Series 2080: Application Considerations

3M positions Wrap Film Series 2080 as a cast vehicle-wrap film with Controltac™ and Comply™ adhesive technologies. Some finishes may include a protective surface layer. Confirm the exact product bulletin for handling, approved surfaces, application temperatures, post-heating, removability, and warranty requirements.

Which printable film fits commercial fleet graphics?

If you are wrapping a commercial cargo van, box truck, or fleet vehicle, you'll be printing custom graphics on printable wrap films rather than using pre-colored rolls. The industry standards for print media are 3M IJ180Cv3 and Avery Dennison MPI 1105.

  • 3M IJ180 family: A printable commercial-graphics family with multiple constructions and compatible overlaminates. Match the precise media, ink, laminate, substrate, and exposure to current 3M documentation.
  • Avery MPI 1105 family: A printable cast-film family with compatible overlaminates and application requirements. Match the precise media, ink, laminate, substrate, and exposure to current Avery Dennison documentation.

3. Durability & The Central Texas Heat

The Central Texas sun is brutal on vinyl wraps. UV radiation and heat can cause vinyl to degrade, dry out, and crack prematurely if low-quality materials are used.

Both 3M and Avery Dennison cast films are designed to withstand high temperatures, but they require proper care and laminate protection. In Austin's climate, a horizontal surface (hood, roof, trunk) receives significantly more UV damage than vertical panels (doors, fenders). Typically:

How should warranty coverage be evaluated?

Warranty coverage depends on the exact film, laminate, ink, printer, substrate, application, exposure, installer eligibility, geography, and current manufacturer terms. Ask for the written warranty that applies to the proposed system before approving production. Do not assume that a product-level warranty automatically covers installation labor or every failure mode.
