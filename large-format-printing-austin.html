What do you need for a print quote?

Gather these details before contacting Canvas. You do not need to choose a material on your own: explain where the graphic will be used so we can review suitable options for that project.

  • Product, finished dimensions and quantity for each size.

YOUR PRINT PROJECT QUESTIONS

Clear specifications before production is confirmed.

What information helps choose the right material?

Share the intended use, application surface, dimensions and installation conditions. Canvas will review the material and finishing for that project before confirming the quote. Not every graphic needs the same film and laminate combination.

Can I request several pieces or designs in one quote?

Yes. Separate quantities by size and design, and identify each vehicle, location or panel. Confirm which pieces need lamination or contour cutting so the production scope is clear before work starts.

How are timing and fulfillment confirmed?

Production timing, pickup and shipping options are confirmed in the quote after specifications and artwork are reviewed. Include your required date and destination ZIP code; confirm availability before scheduling installation.

What if my artwork is not ready yet?

Tell us what you have and what still needs work. Use the preparation checklist to organize dimensions, panels and versions; confirm artwork review and the file-transfer method with Canvas. Do not include confidential information in an initial inquiry.
